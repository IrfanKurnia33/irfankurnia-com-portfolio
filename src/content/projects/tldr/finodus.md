## The problem

Month-end finance reconciliation takes time and manual effort. Finodus explores how AI could reduce that work.

## My contribution

Solo product, design, and engineering in a 4-day sprint, using Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, and the Anthropic Claude API, with a Chrome extension.

## Outcome and status

**Case study in preparation.** The draft “3 days saved” claim still needs measurement evidence. Research, the final experience, and verified outcomes will be added to the full case study.
